# Surface Brief: Ekonomika Web (Hem, Våra Tjänster, Om Oss, Kontakt)

## Mode

Persuade

## Scope & Audience

Four-page Swedish corporate website (`Hem/index.html`, `Tjanster/index.html`, `Om/index.html`, `Kontakt/index.html`) and shared navigation/footer (`General/`) for Ekonomika, a Stockholm-based digital accounting firm benchmarked against Aspia, Ludvig & Co, and Grant Thornton Sverige.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ekonomika presents itself with the calm, institutional credibility of a modern Swedish corporate accounting and advisory firm (Aspia, Ludvig & Co, Grant Thornton Sverige)—replacing boxed grids, monospace badges, and decorative chrome with generous white space, executive forest-green authority, and straightforward service clarity.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white primary canvas (`#FFFFFF`) alternating with soft Nordic sage-mist section bands (`#F4F7F5`), anchored by deep executive forest green (`#183328`) and rich corporate pine (`#244536`), dark slate-forest body ink (`#14241D`), muted secondary copy (`#43564D`), and subtle hairline dividers (`1px solid #E2EAE5`) used only to separate sections and list rows. Typography uses clean corporate sans-serif `Public Sans` (`600`–`700` for headings with `-0.02em` tracking; `400`–`500` for body copy at `60ch–70ch`), with zero kickers/eyebrows, zero monospace badge boxes, and zero heavy outer borders.

STORY: A Swedish business owner (builder, hairdresser, doctor, lawyer, or founder) or private declaration client lands on an immediately trustworthy, recognizable corporate accounting website, sees Ekonomika's four core strengths and tailored services clearly, and contacts the firm with confidence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Clean white corporate header bar with the dark-green Ekonomika wordmark, clear navigation links (`Hem`, `Våra Tjänster`, `Om Oss`), and a solid `#183328` `Kontakt` button, over a full-bleed deep executive forest (`#183328`) corporate hero band. The hero splits cleanly inside a `1200px` container without grid lines: the left column presents the headline ("Byggare, Frisör, Läkare eller Advokat"), calm subcopy ("Vi på Ekonomika tar hand om din ekonomi så du kan slappna av efter jobbet."), and primary/secondary corporate CTAs; the right column features a single, natural-light photograph of a modern Stockholm advisory meeting (`ekonomika-corporate-hero.png`), followed directly below by a clean, borderless 4-pillar trust strip on white.

FORM: Standing Exit / Canon — Modern Swedish Corporate Advisory (benchmarked against Aspia, Ludvig & Co, Grant Thornton Sverige), `seed:d57e80776641bf23`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
